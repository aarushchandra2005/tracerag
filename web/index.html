How to use TraceRAG

TraceRAG checks a scientific claim in three steps: it finds evidence among 5,183 SciFact paper abstracts, writes an answer that cites a passage after every sentence, and checks each sentence against the passage it cites.

  1. Check a claim. Type a claim, or pick an example or a SciFact claim, and press Check claim. Every sentence of the answer gets a verdict. Click a sentence to see its scores and the exact passage it was checked against, highlighted in yellow.
  2. Test the checker. Press Negate it, Swap its citation or Add a fabricated sentence to break one sentence on purpose, or edit the answer yourself and re-check it. A good checker flags the broken sentence.
  3. Compare retrievers. See one claim ranked by tf-idf, BM25, dense retrieval and two hybrids side by side. Papers that SciFact's experts judged relevant are marked; click any paper to see why it scored as it did.
  4. Look inside the index. Follow text through the tokenizer, look up any word's postings list, and recompute a paper's score by hand.
  5. Read the evaluation. All results on the 300 test claims, with interactive charts. In the sparse-vs-dense chart, click a dot to open those claims in the comparison.

The pills in the top bar show what is ready. Right after starting, dense retrieval and the NLI checker may still be loading; tf-idf and BM25 work at once.
